Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how tk-clip (the "Service") handles information when you use it to publish video clips to TikTok. The Service is a personal tool operated by its owner and processes data locally.

1. Information we handle

  • TikTok authorization data. When you connect your account through TikTok's OAuth flow, we receive and store the resulting access_token and refresh_token, your TikTok open_id, the granted scope, and token expiry times.
  • Basic profile information. If the user.info.basic permission is granted, TikTok may return your display name and avatar, which are used only to show which account is connected.
  • Video content and captions. The video files you choose to publish and the captions you provide are processed by the Service and sent to TikTok in order to publish them.
  • Operational logs. The Service may keep local logs of its activity (for example, publication status) for troubleshooting.

2. How we use information

Information is used solely to:

  • Authorize and maintain the connection to your TikTok account.
  • Upload and publish the content you request.
  • Check and report the status of your publications.
  • Keep the Service secure and diagnose errors.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Sharing with third parties

We do not share your information with third parties, except with TikTok to the extent necessary to provide the Service (publishing content and checking status), and where required by law. Your use of TikTok is subject to TikTok's own privacy policy.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored on the operator's own server in a file that is not publicly accessible, and are transmitted to TikTok over HTTPS. Access tokens expire after a short period and are refreshed using the refresh token. We apply reasonable measures to protect this data, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

5. Data retention and deletion

Authorization data is kept while the Service remains connected to your account. You can delete it at any time by:

  • Revoking the Service's access from your TikTok account settings (Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions), which invalidates the tokens; or
  • Using the Service's revoke function, which also removes the stored token file from the server.

Video files and captions are used only to perform the publication you requested and are not retained for any other purpose.

6. Children

The Service is not intended for use by anyone under the minimum age required to use TikTok. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest revision.

8. Contact

For any question about this Privacy Policy or to request deletion of your data, contact simonjuanr@gmail.com.
